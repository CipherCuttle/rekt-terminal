# REKT INK(CUBATOR) — Phase 9 Human Rehearsal v0

**Phase:** PHASE 9 — FOUNDING-COHORT REHEARSAL

**Status:** NOT STARTED

Synthetic fixtures do not satisfy this receipt. This document is completed only from 2–3 real friendly/internal Players using the deployed rehearsal environment.

## Required actors

Use 2–3 real Players. At minimum the rehearsal must exercise the practical roles represented by OWNER, HELPER and TESTER, but one human may perform more than one role where natural.

Do not explain internal implementation phases. Participants should experience the product as a user would.

## Required journey

Each rehearsal must collectively prove that real Players can understand and operate:

1. BECOME — create/login and understand where they are;
2. DECLARE — state what they are shipping and what counts as Ship;
3. BUILD — return to Command and identify current focus;
4. PROVE — understand claimed/observed/proven evidence without authority explanation;
5. HELP — create/discover Help, offer/accept Assist and understand attribution;
6. SHIP — prepare/submit the public artifact and understand verifier/acceptance state;
7. REMEMBER — inspect the durable Ship Receipt/history/reputation outcome;
8. REPEAT — understand how to begin/continue another Mission without corrupting prior history.

## Comprehension prompts

Without coaching, each Player should be able to answer:

- What are you building?
- What counts as Ship?
- What changed recently?
- What is blocking you?
- What should you do next?
- Where can you get or provide help?
- What evidence is claimed, observed or proven?
- What happened after Ship?
